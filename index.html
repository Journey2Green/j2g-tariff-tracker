NERSA schedule and IBT-publication alert

Tracking which metro schedules are officially published versus estimated, so figures can be used with the right level of confidence.

NERSA published Reasons for Decision on all 176 municipal tariff applications for FY2026/27, effective 1 July 2026.
  • Exact FY2026/27 per-block c/kWh for City Power (Johannesburg) and City of Tshwane are not yet in an accessible official PDF, so 2026/27 rates are marked as estimates.
  • eThekwini exact final flat R/kWh at 9% is pending the official PDF.
  • The 2027/28 separately-determined municipal ERTSA percentage has not yet been determined.

Reference: NERSA tariff portal.
